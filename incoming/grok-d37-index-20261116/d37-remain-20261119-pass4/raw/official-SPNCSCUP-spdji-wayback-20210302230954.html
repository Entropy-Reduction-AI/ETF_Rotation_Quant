S&P New China Sectors (A-shares Capped) Index

Overview Data Index-Linked Products News & Research

SPNCSCHP

The S&P New China Sectors (A-shares Capped) Index is designed to measure the performance of China- and Hong Kong-domiciled companies in consumption and service-oriented industries. All Chinese share classes including A-shares and offshore listings are eligible for inclusion. The weight of A-shares is capped at 15% at each semiannual rebalance.
