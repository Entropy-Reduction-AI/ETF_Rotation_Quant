How official free daily PRICE RETURN history works
================================================================
Anonymous access works for current factsheets, Historic Index Values
spreadsheets, and the consolidated ticker-codes XLS. No login wall was
hit on these public Index Resources pages. Nav also lists Client Service
Portal and DDS for Clients (https://data.ftse.com/); those were not
opened (stop-on-login rule).

Observed free-history cap (from the Historic Index Values page itself,
and from the file actually downloaded):
  - Only "a selection" of FTSE Russell indices is offered.
  - Spreadsheets are "generally updated on a quarterly basis with two
    years of month-end values and returns."
  - This is MONTH-END, not daily. It is a short window, not from launch.
  - The downloaded GPCSP007 workbook (file dated 2026-08-19) contains
    25 month-end rows, 2024-07-31 through 2026-07-31.
  - Factsheet page states: "Archived Factsheets are no longer available."
    Current factsheets download anonymously as PDFs. Factsheet performance
    tables for both targets are Total Return (总收益 / Total Return), not
    daily price levels. Factsheets say indices are calculated on price and
    total-return methodologies, but they do not export a daily price series.

Conclusion: official free tools do not provide daily price-return history
from launch. The only free numeric export obtained is a ~2-year monthly
file, and only for a Net Tax code of the Asia Pacific family (see below).
Paid/client data would be via a representative / Client Service / DDS;
those flows were not entered.
